Family mode setup

For setting up ScamTap on a parent's or relative's iPhone. Takes about 5 minutes.

Print this page

Part 1 — you set it up (on their iPhone)

  1. Install ScamTap and open it. In step 1, tap Add Screen Scan shortcut, then Add Shortcut.
  2. Go to Settings › Accessibility › Touch › Back Tap › Double Tap and choose Screen Scan.
  3. Back in ScamTap, finish setup. On "Try it now", double-tap the back of the phone and tap Always Allow.
  4. Open Settings › Family mode. Tap Choose from Contacts and pick yourself, then turn on Family mode.
  5. ScamTap now shows one simple screen. Its settings are behind a "Hold to open" button, so they can't be switched off by accident.

Part 2 — keep this by the phone

Got a message you're not sure about? Don't reply, tap links or pay — check it first:

  1. With the message on screen, tap the back of the phone twice.
  2. Or open ScamTap, copy the message, and tap Paste.
  3. If it says Likely scam or Be careful, tap Ask to send it to me before doing anything.
