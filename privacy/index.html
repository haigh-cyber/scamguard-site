Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

The short version

  • Every check runs on your iPhone. Your messages, screenshots and links are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • ScamTap has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • We (the developer) receive no data from the app.

What ScamTap handles, and where it stays

DataWhat happens to it
Screenshots from Screen Scan, the share sheet or the photo pickerRead on your iPhone to extract the text, then discarded. Never saved.
The camera, when you tap "Scan a QR code"Used live to read the QR code, then turned off. No photo or video is taken or saved. The code's contents are checked on your iPhone.
Message text you checkChecked on your iPhone. Saved in your ScamTap history only if "Save message text in history" is on (you can turn it off).
Check history (verdicts, reasons, dates)Stored on your iPhone and deleted automatically after 30 days by default (7 days, 90 days or never, as you choose). You can delete it any time in Settings.
Phone numbers you blockStored on your iPhone and given to iOS so it can block calls from them.
Family mode helper's name and numberStored on your iPhone and used only to pre-fill a text message draft.
Scan diagnostics (off by default)If you turn this on, the text ScamTap read from recent screen scans (never the image) is kept on your iPhone to help improve accuracy. You can delete it any time.

This data lives in ScamTap's storage on your iPhone. It's included in your normal iPhone or iCloud backups like other app data, and is deleted when you delete the app.

Apple features ScamTap uses

  • Text recognition, QR code reading and ScamTap's scam model run entirely on your iPhone.
  • Apple Intelligence ("Explain in plain English") uses Apple's on-device model.
  • Call blocking uses iOS Call Blocking & Identification; the numbers stay on your device.
  • Shortcuts, widgets and Control Center pass information between iOS and ScamTap on your iPhone.

When something leaves your iPhone

Only when you choose to:

  • Family mode's "Ask" button opens a text message draft to the person you set up. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send in Messages.
  • Report to Scamwatch and Add the Screen Scan shortcut open websites in Safari or Shortcuts. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Children

ScamTap is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
